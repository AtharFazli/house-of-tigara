## Brand & Style

The design system articulates a boutique culinary atelier identity rooted in calm luxury, deliberate craftsmanship, and slow living. It rejects fast-casual loudness in favor of an intimate, high-end editorial publication aesthetic—reminiscent of bespoke culinary folios and curated architectural galleries.

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Organic Tactility**:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Surfaces are unhurried and tactile, avoiding clinical stark whites in favor of linen, alabaster, and warm stone.
- **Rhythm & Poise:** Composition mirrors fine print media, pairing large-scale editorial serif titles with measured, utilitarian body notes.
- **Tone:** Understated, artisanal, composed, and quietly self-assured. Every element functions as part of a gallery exhibition showcasing artisanal flavors and crafted batches.

## Colors

The palette evokes natural botanical ingredients, raw earthenware, and baked patisserie elements.

### Roles & Semantic Values

- **Primary (`#3D4D36` - Deep Olive):** Anchors brand prestige. Applied to high-priority key interactive moments, primary action buttons, key brand banners, and subtle graphic accents. Supporting shades: Forest Shadow (`#2B3825`) for hover/active states, Herbal Sage (`#4A5B42`) for focused borders.
- **Secondary (`#B86548` - Muted Terracotta):** Denotes highlights, limited batch badges, active status indicators, and delicate decorative elements. Supporting tint: Baked Clay (`#C47357`).
- **Tertiary (`#4A3B32` - Rich Espresso):** Grounds structured elements, secondary interactive controls, and editorial subheadings. Deep variant: Roasted Earth (`#2C221E`).
- **Neutral / Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Ivory Alabaster):** The main atmospheric canvas. Surface tint: Warm Linen (`#F5F2EB`) for alternate structural cards and inset sections.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E8E3D9` - Warm Stone):** Subtle separation lines that preserve editorial lightness without stark contrast.
- **Text Primary (`#221C18` - Charcoal Earth):** Maximum contrast reading color; softer and warmer than pure synthetic black.
- **Text Muted (`#6E665D` - Earthy Taupe):** Secondary descriptive details, ingredient provenance notes, captions, and micro-labels.

## Typography

Typographic rhythm depends on the juxtaposition between the romantic elegance of **Playfair Display** and the clean, modernist structure of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.
- **Display & Headlines:** Playfair Display introduces literary character, menu curation, and chapter styling. Headings retain natural optical kerning with subtle negative tracking at large scales.
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures high legibility on mobile screens, retaining open counters and neutral proportions.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to evoke luxury packaging and gallery exhibition plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model mimics an editorial broadsheet combined with a curated Instagram square/portrait grid.
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (1024px+): 12-column balanced grid with a maximum content constraint of `1160px` to maintain focused readability. Uses `gutter` (1.5rem) and generous canvas margin (`margin`: 3rem).
  - Tablet (768px - 1023px): 6-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters.
  - Mobile (< 768px): 2-column or 4-column structure using `gutter-sm` (1rem) and outer margin `margin-sm` (1.25rem).
- **Rhythm & Asymmetry:** Employs asymmetric pairings—such as 7-column editorial image tiles offset by 5-column story text—recreating print magazine spreads. Full-bleed photo tiles alternative with heavily inset content containers to generate visual pace.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth avoids simulated digital dropshadows or heavy skeuomorphic layers. Instead, depth is articulated through **tonal layering**, **micro-borders**, and warm diffused contact shadows:
- **Tonal Layers:** The foundation rests on `#FBF9F5` (Ivory). Inset sections, cards, and modal sheets elevate by shifting to `#F5F2EB` (Warm Linen) or solid contrast `#3D4D36` (Deep Olive).
- **Micro-Borders:** Card edges, separators, and list elements are defined by crisp `1px` borders in `#E8E3D9` (Warm Stone) rather than blur shadows.
- **Ambient Shadowing:** When floating elements require separation (e.g., sticky WhatsApp order bars or floating menus), use an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted aura:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -6px rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.04);`
- **Surface Overlays:** Interactive modal sheets use a gentle backdrop tint of `rgba(34, 28, 24, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

Shapes evoke architectural curves, custom ceramic plates, and artisanal packaging labels.
- Base interactive inputs, buttons, and badges utilize level `2` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) base radius.
- Cards, photo frames, and container blocks scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) for a soft, curated finish.
- Image tiles can selectively use asymmetric radii (e.g., top-left and top-right `rounded-2xl`, bottom flat) to mirror arched classical windows or patisserie showcases.
- Badges and micro-status tags use fully rounded pills for contrast against architectural frame shapes.

## Components

### Buttons & Unified Order CTA

- **Primary CTA ("Pre-order via WhatsApp / DM"):** Deep Olive `#3D4D36` background, `#FBF9F5` text, subtle `1px` border in `#2B3825`. Height 52px, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Text styled with `label-lg` uppercase tracking. Hover state transitions to `#2B3825` with an organic lift.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with `#E8E3D9` border, `#221C18` text, shifting to `#F5F2EB` background on hover.
- **Sticky Pre-order Bar:** Mobile fixed bottom bar using `#FBF9F5` background, top border `#E8E3D9`, containing direct item tally and single unified WhatsApp pre-order button.

### Cards & Grid Tiles

- **Editorial Product Tile:** Framed with `1px` `#E8E3D9` border, `#FBF9F5` or `#F5F2EB` background, `rounded-xl`. Product imagery sits flush with a `4:5` or `1:1` aspect ratio. Typography underneath leads with `headline-sm` in Playfair Display, followed by price in taupe `body-sm`.
- **Story / Journal Tile:** High whitespace canvas with delicate Terracotta (`#B86548`) overline label, brief narrative paragraph, and minimal textual link.

### Chips & Badges

- **Status & Batch Tags ("Limited Batch", "Market Day Special"):** Pill-shaped, `#F5F2EB` fill with a `1px` border of `#E8E3D9`. Text in `#B86548` (Terracotta) using `label-sm`.

### Form Fields & Inputs

- **Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F2EB` background, `1px` border in `#E8E3D9`, `0.5rem` corner radius. Padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state features a crisp `1px` ring in `#3D4D36` with no aggressive glow. Placeholder text in `#6E665D`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors

- Custom circles and rounded squares with a `1.5px` border in `#6E665D`. When selected, filled with `#3D4D36` displaying an Ivory `#FBF9F5` check mark.

### Lists & Menu Tiers

- **Boutique Menu Row:** Clean horizontal layout separated by a hairline `1px` border `#E8E3D9`. Item title in Playfair Display (`headline-sm`), dotted or open editorial spacing, and price aligned right in Charcoal Earth (`#221C18`). Subtext features tasting notes in Taupe (`#6E665D`).
